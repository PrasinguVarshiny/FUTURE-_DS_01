# Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Task 1
## Business Sales Performance Analytics

### 📌 Project Overview
This project was completed as part of **Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Task 1**.

The objective is to analyze business sales data and create a client-ready dashboard that helps identify:

- Revenue and sales performance
- Top-selling products
- Category performance
- Regional performance
- Profitability and profit margins
- Areas where the business can improve and grow

### 🛠️ Tool Used
**Tableau**

Tableau was used for data analysis, visualization, KPI creation, and dashboard development.

### 📊 Dataset
The project uses the **Sample Superstore** sales dataset.

The uploaded dataset contains **9,994 rows** and **21 columns**.

Key business fields include sales, profit, quantity, product, category, sub-category, region, and order information.

### 📈 Dashboard KPIs
The dashboard includes:

- **Total Sales:** 2,297,201
- **Total Profit:** 286,397
- **Total Quantity:** 37,873
- **Profit Margin:** 12.47%

### 📊 Dashboard Analysis
The Tableau dashboard contains:

1. **Category Sales** – compares sales across product categories.
2. **Category Profit** – compares profitability across categories.
3. **Region Sales** – analyzes sales performance by region.
4. **Region Profit** – analyzes profitability by region.
5. **Sub-Category Sales** – compares sales across sub-categories.
6. **Sub-Category Profit** – identifies profitable and loss-making sub-categories.
7. **Product Sales** – identifies top-selling products.

### 💡 Business Insights
The analysis is designed to identify:

- Categories contributing strongly to revenue.
- Regions with stronger or weaker sales and profit performance.
- Sub-categories generating high sales versus high profit.
- Products that contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Products or sub-categories that require profitability monitoring.

### 🎯 Actionable Recommendations
Based on the dashboard findings, recommendations can include:

- Focus growth efforts on high-performing categories and regions.
- Monitor products with high sales but relatively low profit.
- Investigate sub-categories with low or negative profit.
- Use regional performance to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Track Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Profit Margin regularly.
